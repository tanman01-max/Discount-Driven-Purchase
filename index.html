How it works

Two models, one rule-based score.

01 DATASessions with offer, behaviour and outcome.
02 CLEANValidate, encode, keep discounted purchases.
03 FEATURESFive behaviour signals, standardised.
04 SCOREFive-factor deal-seeker score from 0 to 5.
05 SCREENIsolation Forest flags unusual purchases.
06 PREDICTGradient Boosting scores each purchase.
